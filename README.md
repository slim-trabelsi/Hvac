# 🎂 Happy 21st Birthday Website (Tunisia 🇹🇳 to Cebu, Philippines 🇵🇭)

A romantic, personalized 21st birthday website built specifically for your girlfriend in Cebu!

## ✨ Included Features:
1. **Direct Birthday Song on Open ("Happy Birthday to You" + Harana Chime)**:
   - Greeting card: *"☀️ Good morning & Mata na, Langga! A special birthday surprise sent all the way from Tunisia to Cebu just for you..."*
   - Tapping the button immediately plays the **"Happy Birthday to You"** song aloud with zero lag.
   - Interactive music player in the top corner to toggle playback anytime.

2. **Tailored Long-Distance Story (Tunisia 🇹🇳 to Cebu 🇵🇭)**:
   - Celebrating your bond across 10,000+ km without ever having met yet.
   - Beautiful English with sweet Cebuano (Bisaya) terms of endearment (*Palangga*, *Langga*, *Mata na*, *Malipayong ika-21 nga Adlawng Natawhan*, *Gihigugma tika kaayo*).

3. **Interactive 21st Birthday Milestone Cake**:
   - 3-tier birthday cake with candles she can blow out.
   - Plays a sweet blowing sound effect, pops celebratory confetti, and reveals your personal birthday blessing.

4. **The Secret 3D Gift Box & Wax-Sealed Love Letter**:
   - Tapping the wrapped gift box opens the letter modal with a wax seal.
   - Clean, heartfelt prose thanking her for her sweetness and patience across the miles, with **no underscores or blanks**.
   - Includes a "Save Letter" button.

5. **21 Reasons Why I Love You**:
   - 21 interactive cards (one for every year of her life) that flip when tapped.
   - Updated with genuine moments: Your Radiant Smile, Your Sweet Voice Messages, The Way You Check Up on Me, Your Beautiful Pictures, Your Pure Heart, and more.

6. **"Kilig" Meter & Playful Mini-Quiz**:
   - Interactive love gauge that overflows to 1,000,000% at maximum.
   - Playful quiz with a runaway "No" button so she can only say "Yes!".

Simply double-click `index.html` to open it in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or any browser!
